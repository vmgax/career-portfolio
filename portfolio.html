Academic

Unstructured Hiring

Undergraduate thesis · De La Salle University

A qualitative case study of how Filipino AI data laborers are recruited through Facebook and Reddit — and how informal recruitment produces precarious work.

  • Qualitative research
  • Case study
  • Policy analysis

Overview

Generative AI has driven rapid growth in AI freelancing, opening work to Filipino workers through platforms such as Facebook and Reddit. The study examines how those workers are recruited on social networking sites, and how the resulting informal arrangements contribute to precarious work.

Research questions

  • How are Facebook and Reddit used to recruit Filipino AI data laborers — in posting frequency, types of companies, and recruitment structures?
  • What draws Filipinos into AI data labor despite informal arrangements, low pay and limited protections?
  • How does the lack of regulatory policy in the Philippine AI and digital labor sector normalize informal recruitment?

Findings

Facebook and Reddit function as genuinely accessible recruitment spaces — workers are drawn by flexible schedules, remote work, easy applications and immediate income. The same postings routinely lack written contracts, clear payment terms, benefits and grievance mechanisms, leaving workers exposed to unstable and potentially exploitative conditions.

Recommendations

Clearer government labor policy for AI data work and platform-based employment; full disclosure of wages, tasks, contracts and worker rights in recruitment posts; and shared accountability between platforms, employers and workers for safer, more transparent recruitment.

Proponents

Tiu Sonco, Francis John M. · Baldago, Hannah Margarita I. · Diaz de Rivera, Anika Beatriz D.

Civic Engagement

Re-Leaf the Heat!

Habitat for Humanity — Green Chapter · Project member

An urban greening initiative against extreme heat that placed in the Top 3 of more than 80 entries and won a USD 4,000 grant.

  • Project development
  • Climate resilience
  • Community engagement

Overview

Re-Leaf the Heat! tackles extreme heat in Metro Manila’s informal settlements by installing vertical green walls on 30 homes. The natural cooling system reduces indoor temperatures, promotes sustainable living and improves public health.

Approach

Hands-on community training so residents gain skills in installing and maintaining the walls, while learning the environmental benefits of green infrastructure — turning homes into living walls rather than delivering a one-off intervention.

Results

  • 30 households benefit directly.
  • Over 150 individuals engaged through education and outreach.
  • Indirect impact reaching up to 500 community members.
  • Top 3 of 80+ entries at Habitat Youth Solutions 2025; USD 4,000 grant won.

My role

Project member, working under project leads Alexandra Taladua and Ginger Swa alongside eight other members.
