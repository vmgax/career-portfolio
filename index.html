Hello

Who I am and where I am studying.

Hello! I am Anika Diaz de Rivera. Currently, I am an undergraduate student taking a Bachelor of Arts in Development Studies and a minor in Marketing Strategy at De La Salle University in Manila, Philippines.

My work sits at the meeting point of project management, event planning, advocacy and human resources — built across a student publication, three student organisations, a volunteer chapter, and an internship with the Philippine Chamber of Commerce and Industry. I am experienced in managing and creating efficiency tools and systems using Google Workspace, with an adaptable skill set across corporate, advocacy and institutional settings.

What I am working toward is a place in something genuinely great — an institution like the United Nations, where the scale matches the ambition. I want to serve the community I come from and leave behind an initiative that outlasts my involvement in it.

Specialty in event planning — formal, informal, and advocacy-oriented. — Anika Diaz de Rivera
